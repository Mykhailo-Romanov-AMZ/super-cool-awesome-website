Chapter III — Inspection Procedure

Standard inspections occur without notice and at the inspector's discretion. The following is the official procedure and is not subject to negotiation.

  1. Scheduled Observation

    The inspector (a cat) positions themselves in a location of maximum visibility.

  2. Sofa & Box Audit

    All resting surfaces are tested. Findings are final and binding.

  3. Temperament Notation

    Attitude is recorded. Favorable notes affect future treat allocations.

  4. Official Stamping

    A pawprint is applied to the report. The inspector then departs for a nap.

Note: inspectors have never been observed by the public. Inspectors are cats.
